# Returns

## Starting a return

Start a return from Account, then Orders, then Return. Choose the reason and the item, and we issue an RMA number within 1 business day.

The parcel must be postmarked within 10 days of the RMA number being issued. After that the RMA expires and you need to open a new one. Write the RMA number on the outside of the box.

## Condition requirements

Returned items must include every accessory that shipped in the box: the charging cable, the case, the ear tips, and the documentation. Missing accessories may reduce the refund.

Do not write on the product box. A box covered in tape or marker is treated as damaged packaging and a 15% restocking fee applies.

## Products that cannot be returned

Ear tips and in-ear products cannot be returned once the hygiene seal is broken, except when they are defective.

Gift cards, digital downloads and clearance items marked final sale cannot be returned.

## Damaged or wrong items

Report an item that arrived damaged, or an order that contained the wrong item, within 48 hours of delivery. Attach a photo of the item and the shipping box to the support ticket. We ship a replacement immediately and do not ask you to return the damaged item for orders under 100 USD.

## Refused and undeliverable parcels

If a parcel is returned to us as undeliverable, we refund the item price but not the original shipping cost. If you refuse a parcel that was not damaged, the return is treated as a normal return and the 8.95 USD return label cost applies.
