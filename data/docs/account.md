# Account and privacy

## Signing in

A password reset link is valid for 30 minutes and can be used once. If it expires, request a new link from the sign-in page.

Two-factor authentication can be enabled with an authenticator app or by SMS. We recommend the authenticator app. Recovery codes are shown once when 2FA is enabled and should be stored offline.

## Changing your details

Changing your email address requires confirmation from both the old and the new address. The change takes effect after 24 hours.

A delivery address can be edited until the order reaches the packing stage. After that the address cannot be changed and the parcel must be returned or redirected by the carrier.

Accounts cannot be merged. Orders, warranty registrations and store credit stay with the account that placed them.

## Deleting your account

Delete your account from Settings, then Privacy, then Delete account. Deletion has a 30-day grace period, during which signing in cancels the request.

After 30 days we delete your profile, addresses and payment methods. Order records are kept for 7 years because tax law requires them, but they are detached from your profile.

## Exporting your data

You can download a copy of your data from Settings, then Privacy, then Export. The export is prepared within 30 days and sent as a download link valid for 7 days.
