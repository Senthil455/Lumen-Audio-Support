# Warranty

Every Lumen Audio product includes a limited warranty against manufacturing defects.

## Warranty periods

Lumen Buds Pro and Lumen Studio Over-Ear carry a 2-year limited warranty from the delivery date. Lumen Buds Lite and Lumen Mini Speaker carry a 1-year limited warranty.

The warranty applies to the original purchaser and cannot be transferred. Proof of purchase or an order number is required.

## What the warranty covers

The warranty covers manufacturing defects, a charging case that will not hold charge, a driver that fails without physical damage, and battery capacity that drops below 80% of the original rating within the warranty period.

The warranty does not cover accidental damage, cracked housings, damage from liquid beyond the product's IP rating, loss, theft, or cosmetic wear such as scuffs and stretched headbands.

## Making a claim

Start a warranty claim from Account, then Orders, then Warranty. Enter the serial number, which is printed on the box and shown in the Lumen app under Device details.

We inspect the returned item within 5 business days of arrival. If the claim is approved we repair or replace the item at our discretion and ship the replacement within 3 business days of the inspection.

## Out-of-warranty repairs

If the item is outside warranty or the damage is not covered, you can still pay for a repair. The flat repair fees are 49 USD for Lumen Buds Pro, 39 USD for Lumen Buds Lite, 79 USD for Lumen Studio Over-Ear and 39 USD for Lumen Mini Speaker. The fee includes return shipping.

## International warranty

The warranty is valid in the 12 countries we ship to, through local service partners. International warranty claims may take up to 15 business days because of shipping and customs. Customers outside the United States are responsible for shipping the item to the regional service centre.
