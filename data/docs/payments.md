# Payments and billing

## Accepted payment methods

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, Apple Pay and Google Pay.

Klarna is available for orders over 100 USD in the United States and splits the total into 4 interest-free instalments. Klarna is not available on gift cards.

## When you are charged

Your card is charged when the order ships, not when it is placed. Placing an order creates an authorisation hold that usually disappears within 1 to 7 business days.

If part of an order ships later, the charge is split and each shipment is charged separately.

## Currency and international payments

All prices on the website are in US dollars. International customers are charged in US dollars and the bank converts the amount using its own rate. Lumen Audio does not add a currency conversion fee.

Local taxes and import duties are shown separately at checkout only for orders inside the United States. Outside the United States the carrier collects duties on delivery.

## Declined payments

If a payment is declined, the order is held for 24 hours and then cancelled automatically. Check that the billing address matches the card, then place the order again.

A declined authorisation is not a charge. If your statement shows a charge for an order that was cancelled, contact support with the order number and we release it.

## Invoices and disputes

An invoice is emailed when the order ships and is also available in Account, then Orders. Business customers can add a VAT number at checkout.

If you disagree with a charge, contact support before opening a chargeback. We resolve billing disputes within 10 business days.
