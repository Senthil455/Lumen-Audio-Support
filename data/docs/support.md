# Contacting support

## Hours and channels

Human support is available Monday to Friday, 9am to 6pm Eastern Time. Chat has an automated assistant outside those hours.

Phone support is available in the United States and Canada on 1-888-555-0142 during opening hours. International customers are supported by email only, because phone lines are not staffed for other time zones.

Email is support@lumenaudio.example and is answered within 4 business hours.

## Response times

The first reply to an email or chat is sent within 4 business hours. A warranty decision is sent within 5 business days of the item arriving at our inspection centre.

Billing disputes are resolved within 10 business days. A lost parcel trace takes up to 10 business days because it depends on the carrier.

## Escalation

If the first reply does not solve the problem, reply to the same ticket and ask for an escalation. A specialist reviews escalated tickets within 1 business day.

Every ticket has a reference in the form LA-XXXXXX. Keep the reference in all follow-up messages so the history stays in one place.

## What support cannot do

Support cannot change a shipping address after an order reaches packing, cannot cancel a parcel already with the carrier, and cannot issue a refund to a different payment method from the one used for the order.

Support never asks for your password, your full card number or a one-time code. Any message that does is not from Lumen Audio.
