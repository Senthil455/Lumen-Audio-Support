# Shipping and delivery

All orders ship from our warehouse in Columbus, Ohio. The daily cut-off is 2pm Eastern Time on business days. We do not ship on Saturdays, Sundays or US public holidays.

## Domestic shipping options

Standard shipping is free on orders over 75 USD and 5.95 USD otherwise. Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days.

Express shipping costs 14.95 USD and arrives in 1 to 2 business days when the order is placed before the 2pm ET cut-off.

Same-day delivery is available in New York City, Los Angeles and Chicago for 24.95 USD on orders placed before 11am local time. Same-day delivery is not available on Sundays.

## Remote US addresses

Deliveries to Alaska, Hawaii and US territories cost an extra 12 USD and take 5 to 7 business days. Same-day and express shipping are not available at these addresses.

## International shipping

We ship to 12 countries: Canada, the United Kingdom, Ireland, Germany, France, Spain, Italy, the Netherlands, Sweden, Australia, New Zealand and Japan. International shipping costs 19.95 USD and takes 7 to 14 business days.

Import duties, VAT and customs handling fees are the customer's responsibility and are collected by the carrier, not by Lumen Audio.

## Tracking and delays

A tracking number is emailed within 24 hours of dispatch. If tracking has not updated for 5 business days, contact support and we open a trace with the carrier.

A parcel is considered lost if it has not moved for 10 business days, and we replace it at no cost once the carrier confirms the trace.
