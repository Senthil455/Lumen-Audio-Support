# Promotions and discounts

## Student discount

Students get 15% off with a verified student account. Verification is handled by our partner and must be renewed every 12 months.

The student discount cannot be combined with sale items or with the Black Friday event. It can be used with regular-priced items only.

## Coupons and stacking

At most two coupons can be applied to one order. The combined discount cannot exceed 30% of the order value. Coupons cannot be applied after the order has shipped.

Coupons have an expiry date, are single use unless stated otherwise, and cannot be exchanged for cash.

## Price drops

If an item drops in price within 14 days of delivery, we refund the difference once per order. Price adjustments do not apply to flash sales, limited drops or Black Friday pricing.

## Black Friday and holiday returns

The Black Friday event runs from 24 to 30 November. Prices during the event are final and are not eligible for price adjustments.

Purchases made between 24 November and 24 December can be returned until 15 January, even though the standard refund window would have expired.

## Referral credit

When you refer a friend, both accounts receive 20 USD in store credit after the friend's first order over 50 USD. Referral credit cannot be used to buy gift cards and does not count towards the free shipping threshold.

A maximum of 10 referrals per account per year is allowed.
