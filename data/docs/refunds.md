# Refund policy

Lumen Audio sells direct to customers in the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom and nine other countries. This policy explains when a refund is available and how long it takes.

## Refund windows

Unused items in their original packaging can be returned for a refund within 30 days of delivery. Opened items can be returned within 14 days of delivery, and a 15% restocking fee applies to opened items.

Orders older than 30 days are not eligible for a refund. Defects found after 30 days are handled under the warranty instead of the refund policy.

## How refunds are paid

Refunds are issued to the original payment method. We process the refund within 5 business days of receiving the returned item, but the bank usually needs another 5 to 10 business days to post it to your statement.

Gift purchases are refunded as store credit to the recipient, not to the buyer's card. Store credit does not expire.

## Return shipping cost

Return shipping is free when the item is defective or we shipped the wrong item. For all other returns we deduct 8.95 USD from the refund for a prepaid US return label.

International returns are always paid by the customer, and any customs fees already paid are not refunded.

## Refund exceptions

Bundles must be returned complete. If one item from a bundle is missing, we refund the bundle price minus the full list price of the missing item.

Ear tips and in-ear products cannot be refunded once the hygiene seal is broken, except when they are defective.

## Price adjustments

If the price of an item drops within 14 days of delivery we refund the difference once per order. Price adjustments exclude flash sales and are not available on Black Friday purchases.
