# Product catalogue

Lumen Audio sells four products. All of them pair with the Lumen app for firmware updates and the serial number lookup.

## Product line

| Model | Price (USD) | Battery life | Case battery | Weight | Water resistance | Noise cancelling |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lumen Buds Pro | 179 | 8 hours | 24 hours | 5.2 g | IPX4 | Yes |
| Lumen Buds Lite | 89 | 6 hours | 18 hours | 4.8 g | IPX4 | No |
| Lumen Studio Over-Ear | 299 | 40 hours | not applicable | 265 g | IPX2 | Yes |
| Lumen Mini Speaker | 59 | 12 hours | not applicable | 340 g | IP67 | No |

## Lumen Buds Pro

The flagship in-ear model with adaptive noise cancelling, multipoint pairing and wireless charging. It is the only model with a replaceable battery service.

## Lumen Buds Lite

The entry-level in-ear model. It has the same fit as the Buds Pro but no noise cancelling and no wireless charging case.

## Lumen Studio Over-Ear

A full-size headphone with 40 hours of playback, memory foam ear cushions and a detachable 3.5 mm cable. The ear cushions are user replaceable and cost 29 USD for a pair.

## Lumen Mini Speaker

A portable speaker with an IP67 rating, a strap and USB-C charging. It can be paired with a second Mini Speaker for stereo sound. It floats in water.
